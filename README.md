## 🚀 About

Edviora is a modern School ERP platform designed to help educational institutions manage their daily operations efficiently.

Our mission is to provide schools with secure, scalable, and intelligent software that reduces administrative workload while improving communication between schools, teachers, students, and parents.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📚 Student Information Management
- ✅ Smart Attendance Management
- 📩 Automatic Parent Notifications
- 💰 Fee Management
- 📝 Examination & Marks Management
- 👨‍🏫 Staff Management
- 📅 School Calendar
- 🚌 Transport Management
- 🤖 AI Assistant
- 📊 Analytics & Reports
- 🔐 Role-Based Access Control
